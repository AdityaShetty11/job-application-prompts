# Prompt: Tailored CV

Create a clear, truthful CV for the job described below, tailored to the applicant's profile.

## Inputs

### Job description

Paste the complete job posting here:

`[JOB DESCRIPTION]`

### Applicant profile

Paste the applicant's current CV and any additional relevant information here. Include only details the applicant is comfortable sharing:

`[APPLICANT PROFILE]`

### Preferences (optional)

Specify the target country or market, preferred length, spelling convention, or other formatting requirements:

`[PREFERENCES]`

## Instructions

- Use the applicant profile as the sole source of facts. Do not invent or infer employers, dates, degrees, certifications, skills, responsibilities, achievements, metrics, or proficiency levels.
- Tailor the summary, skills, and experience bullets to the job's actual requirements. Prioritize relevant, supported experience and use terminology from the posting where it accurately describes the applicant.
- Preserve the meaning and scope of the applicant's experience. Do not turn participation into ownership or imply a qualification the profile does not establish.
- Keep claims specific and concise. Retain metrics only when present in the profile; do not estimate or manufacture numbers.
- If an essential detail is missing or unclear, leave it out and list a concise clarification question after the CV rather than guessing.
- Use a simple, readable, ATS-friendly structure with standard headings. Do not use tables, columns, icons, graphics, or keyword stuffing.
- Include only sections supported by the profile. Order experience and education in reverse chronological order when dates are available.
- Follow the optional preferences when provided. Otherwise use a clean, professional format suitable for the role and market.

## Output

Return the finished CV first, followed only by any necessary clarification questions. Do not include claims that are unsupported by the applicant profile.
