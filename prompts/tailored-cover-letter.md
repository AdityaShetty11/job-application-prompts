# Prompt: Tailored Cover Letter

Write a concise, specific cover letter for the job described below, grounded in the applicant's profile.

## Inputs

### Job description

Paste the complete job posting here:

`[JOB DESCRIPTION]`

### Applicant profile

Paste the applicant's current CV and any additional relevant information here. Include only details the applicant is comfortable sharing:

`[APPLICANT PROFILE]`

### Preferences (optional)

Specify the hiring manager's name, company or role naming preferences, tone, length, spelling convention, or other requirements:

`[PREFERENCES]`

## Instructions

- Use the applicant profile as the sole source of facts. Do not invent personal motivations, relationships, achievements, qualifications, employers, dates, or results.
- Connect the employer's actual needs in the job description to the applicant's most relevant, supported experience. Explain the fit with concrete examples rather than repeating the posting or listing keywords.
- Preserve the applicant's actual level of responsibility and the scope of each accomplishment. Do not overstate experience or imply skills that the profile does not establish.
- Do not assume a hiring manager's name or other missing details. Use a neutral greeting or omit the greeting if no name is supplied.
- Keep the letter concise, professional, and natural. Avoid generic praise, clichés, unsupported claims, and unnecessary repetition of the CV.
- Follow the optional preferences when provided. Otherwise use a suitable professional tone and a brief, readable format.
- If a detail needed to make the letter accurate is missing or unclear, avoid guessing and ask a concise clarification question after the letter.

## Output

Return the finished cover letter first, followed only by any necessary clarification questions. Do not include claims that are unsupported by the applicant profile.
